Adobe MCP Client

Target & Analytics

Connect your Adobe MCP servers

This app talks to Adobe Experience Cloud through Adobe's Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers. Connect Adobe Target and/or Adobe Analytics — each signs in separately with Adobe IMS OAuth 2.0 (no static credentials are stored). Once connected, browse tools or ask the assistant in plain language.

Access is governed by your Adobe product roles (e.g. Target Observer / Editor / Approver, or Analytics report-suite permissions).
