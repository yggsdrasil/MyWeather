Connect to Adobe Target

This app talks to Adobe Target through Adobe's Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. Click Connect to sign in with your Adobe Experience Cloud account. Authentication uses Adobe IMS OAuth 2.0 — no static credentials are stored by Adobe.

  1. Click Connect.
  2. Authorize with Adobe IMS in the popup / new tab.
  3. Browse the available tools and run experimentation & personalization queries.

Authorization is required to continue.
